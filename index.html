About Me

Welcome to my portfolio. I am an aspiring software developer with the ambition to become a Fullstack Developer.

I take a structured approach to learning and follow a clearly defined development path. My current tech stack is built on strong frontend fundamentals: HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. The next step in my journey is to seamlessly transition into working with TypeScript, React, and backend technologies.

Although my professional background is in a different industry, I bring an analytical mindset and a holistic perspective on technology. I actively follow trends in artificial intelligence, cybersecurity, and process automation. I strongly believe that understanding the broader architectural context is key to writing better, more secure, and highly scalable code. The primary goal of my learning process and this portfolio is to launch my professional career in IT. Simultaneously, I am striving to acquire the expertise needed to independently design and deploy my own applications and solutions.
